# Signal House

> Signal House is a LinkedIn growth agency for B2B founders and senior executives. We turn LinkedIn into a credible business asset through profile optimisation, ghostwritten content, company page management, lead generation, personal branding strategy, and coaching. Done-with-you, never templated — a real team runs your profile, content and outreach with you, in your own voice.

## Who this is for

Founders, CEOs and senior executives at B2B companies (SaaS, consulting, financial services, social enterprise) who know LinkedIn matters for pipeline and credibility, but don't have the time, writing ability, or system to run it well themselves. Signal House is not a course, a template, or a ghostwriting-only service — it is a done-with-you system covering profile, content, and outreach together.

## How it works (process)

1. **Audit & Strategy** — Read the founder's profile, audience and goals, then agree the position that sets them apart in their category.
2. **Optimise the Profile** — Rewrite headline, About, banner and featured section so visitors see authority and reach out.
3. **Content & Outreach** — Run posting and outreach in the founder's voice, consistent and aimed at the people they want to meet.
4. **Inbound & Pipeline** — Inbound messages and booked calls build up over 60-90 days; Signal House doubles down on what's working.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this a course or a template?**
Neither. A real team works on your profile, content and outreach with you — you are not handed a PDF and left to figure it out.

**How much of my time does it take?**
About 30-45 minutes a week, usually one short call to talk through ideas. Signal House handles the writing, scheduling, outreach and reporting.

Always the founder's own. Signal House interviews them, learns how they talk, and writes in their voice — every post is approved before it goes live.

**How quickly will I see results?**
Profile and positioning change in week one. Reach and inbound conversations typically build over the first 60-90 days.
