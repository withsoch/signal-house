Most advice on the linkedin connection request limit treats it as a volume problem: how to send more, faster, around the cap. We run founder and executive accounts every day, and the cap almost never binds for them.

It binds when invites go to a list. It rarely binds when invites go to people who already saw the founder's content. The number matters less than who you spend it on.

Here is how the limit works, which figures are estimates, and what changes in the inbox when requests follow engagement instead of a spreadsheet.

## How many LinkedIn connection requests can you send per week?

LinkedIn does not publish a fixed weekly connection request limit. Third-party estimates commonly put it around 100 invitations per week, though the threshold can vary by account and behaviour.

![Hand holding a smartphone above a desk in a bright office](/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit-targeting-problem-inline-1.jpg)

*Your own weekly ceiling is a range that can shift, so watch for the warning instead of trusting a number.*

Almost every page ranking for this topic states a single figure as fact. LinkedIn does not. The numbers here come from third-party sources observing account behaviour, and the exact calculation is undisclosed.

In practice that means your own ceiling may sit above or below the average, and it can shift. Plan around a range, and watch for the warning message instead of trusting a number.

## When does the weekly limit reset?

The limit resets on a rolling seven-day window counted from your first sent invitation, not at the start of the calendar week. There is no Monday morning refresh.

![Wall calendar with marked days beside a desk and laptop](/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit-targeting-problem-inline-2.jpg)

*The window rolls from your first invite, so steady daily pacing beats a Monday burst.*

An example: you send a burst of invitations on Monday and hit the cap by Tuesday. Capacity does not return on Sunday night. It comes back as that first Monday batch ages past seven days, so you may be locked out for most of the week and then have room again almost at once.

That is why pace matters more than the weekly total. Most experts recommend staying under about 20 to 25 requests per day, because a sudden spike looks like spam to the filters. A steady handful each day reads as a person. A Monday burst reads as a tool.

## Why does LinkedIn cap requests, and what changes with a note, a free account or Premium?

The cap exists to limit spam. LinkedIn wants invitations to go to people who might accept them, and it uses ignored invites and "I don't know this person" reports as signals.

Notes are capped more tightly. Free accounts are estimated to get around 5 personalized connection notes per month.

Paid tiers do not remove the weekly cap on invitations. What Sales Navigator and InMail change is reach: InMail can message non-connections without an invite, but it runs on credits. Connection requests are the opposite: limited in volume and weak for long pitches.

**A note is for context, not a pitch.** One line on why you are connecting does more than a paragraph about your company.

## What happens when you hit the limit, and what about the 30,000 connection ceiling?

These are two different caps, and they are often blurred together.

### The weekly block

Hit the weekly limit and LinkedIn tells you that you have reached it. It is temporary. Wait for the rolling window to clear and you can send again.

### The 30,000 connection ceiling

At 30,000 connections you can no longer send or accept new connection requests. Connect turns into Follow, and you grow by acquiring followers instead. Few founders get near it, and it has nothing to do with the weekly cap.

### A limit is not a restriction

A limit is a pause of about a week. A restriction is an account penalty, and repeated Terms of Service violations can lead to a permanent ban. The usual cause is automation. We do not use bypass tools or bulk automation on any account, because the downside is losing the profile that the whole pipeline depends on.

## How do founders stay under the limit without losing pipeline?

Spend invites on people who already know your name. Send requests to those who viewed your profile or engaged with the founder's posts: a like, a comment, a repost. They have seen your content, so the request is a continuation, not an interruption.

![Two colleagues talking over a laptop at a cafe table](/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit-targeting-problem-inline-3.jpg)

*Invites go furthest when they follow a like or comment from someone who already knows your name.*

The effect shows up in the inbox, not in a dashboard. Accepted invites turn into short replies that reference a post, and fewer requests sit ignored. Fewer ignored invites also protect the account's standing, so the cap stays comfortable.

A first message after acceptance can be as plain as this example: "Thanks for connecting. You commented on the post about hiring your first sales lead, and I would like to hear how you decided." It asks about them, not about us.

### Withdraw stale pending requests

Review old pending invitations periodically, but don't withdraw them expecting to regain invitation capacity immediately.

### Where outreach goes when invites run out

When the week's invites are spent, do not wait. Use the channels that never touched the cap:

- **Warm introductions.** Ask a mutual connection to make the introduction.
- **Comments.** A useful comment on a prospect's post puts you in front of them without an invite.
- **Email.** If you already have the address through a legitimate route, a short note works.

## FAQ

### How many LinkedIn connection requests can I send per day?

LinkedIn publishes no daily number. Most experts suggest staying under about 20 to 25 per day, since the cap runs on a weekly rolling window and sudden spikes can trigger spam filters. Steady pacing is safer than sending everything at once.

### What happens when you reach 30,000 connections on LinkedIn?

You can no longer send or accept new connection requests. The Connect button becomes Follow, and you grow your reach by gaining followers instead. This is separate from the weekly invitation cap, which is temporary and resets.

### How do I get around the LinkedIn connection request limit?

We do not recommend bypass tools, because automation is the main cause of account restrictions. Send fewer, better-targeted invites to people who engaged with your content, withdraw stale pending requests, and use introductions, comments and email for everything else.

## Spend the invites where they get answered

The weekly cap is real, but it is a constraint on lists, not on people who already read your posts. Send requests after engagement, and the limit rarely matters.
